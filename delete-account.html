Delete your account

A ReefRoutine account only exists if you signed in to Cloud Sync. Without one, all your data is on your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

In the app

  1. Open ReefRoutine and go to Settings → Cloud Sync.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email bastian.tangedal@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We do this within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address and display name.
  • Your household membership. If you were the only member, the household's synced records and photos are deleted too.
  • If other people are still in your household, the shared aquarium records stay with them, because they are their records too.

What is kept

  • Records on your own device stay there until you delete the app.
  • Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat follow their own retention rules, which tax and accounting law may require.
